## Rayhan Mundra

Computer Science + Biomedical Engineering at Johns Hopkins. I like building things that ship — iOS apps, custom hardware, and AI tooling people actually use.

### Currently

- **[Peak-Peek](https://github.com/rayray2006/Peak-Peek)** — iOS app that names the mountains around you, in a 3D terrain view and live AR. 3D engine written from scratch in Metal over satellite elevation data. [On the App Store](https://apple.co/4zNPQrr).
- **[JHU Course Planner](https://github.com/rayray2006/JHU-Course-Planner)** — plain-English course planning for Hopkins students, with a tool-calling agent that screens prerequisites and builds conflict-free schedules. [Live](https://jhu-course-chat.vercel.app).
- **Bike computer** — custom PCBs and firmware for a handlebar cycling computer, pairing over ANT+ and BLE.
- Undergraduate TA for *Machine Learning: Deep Learning*.

### Selected work

- **[ASTRA](https://github.com/rayray2006/ASTRA)** — robotic surgical assistant built with JHU BME and practicing surgeons. A 6-DOF arm that recognizes, grasps, and hands instruments to a surgeon in real time. Presented at BMES.
- **[Albedo](https://github.com/rayray2006/Albedo)** — window-level solar irradiance on buildings, from Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles.
- **[Cued](https://github.com/rayray2006/Cued)** — watchOS app that taps your wrist when a coding agent needs you.
- **[Reminisce](https://github.com/rayray2006/Reminisce)** — reminiscence-therapy companion for families living with dementia.
